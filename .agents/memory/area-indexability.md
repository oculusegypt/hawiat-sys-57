---
name: Area indexability
description: The safe SEO policy for neighborhood compatibility routes and regional landing pages.
---

Only the five regional Riyadh landing pages are currently indexable. Individual neighborhood routes remain available for visitors and legacy links, but must be `noindex, follow` and excluded from the sitemap and indexable SEO inventory.

**Why:** The neighborhood pages share a common template and do not yet contain enough verified, neighborhood-specific information to justify a large doorway-page network. Keeping the routes live avoids broken links while preventing near-duplicate pages from competing in search.

**How to apply:** Any future change to area indexability must update the shared area route policy, prerendered HTML, runtime React metadata, sitemap generation, and SEO inventory together, then run the full SEO gate.
